Cheesecake Factory Restaurant Near Me: Locations, Hours, Ordering, and What to Expect

Find the closest Cheesecake Factory, check today's hours, decide between dine-in, pickup, or delivery, and get a quick preview of prices and the menu before you go. It's updated for July 2026, when the chain operates roughly 218 restaurants across the U.S. plus 35 international locations.

Hours, wait times, and a few menu prices shift by market, especially in California, the Northeast, and Hawaii. Use the official location finder for the exact address, phone number, and hours of your nearest restaurant, since mall-based locations often close later than standalone buildings.

How to find your nearest Cheesecake Factory

Searching "Cheesecake Factory near me" usually means you want an address fast, not a history lesson. Here's the quickest path, plus what to check once you land on a location page so you're not surprised by a closed dining room or a 90-minute wait.

  • Open the official location finder at thecheesecakefactory.com/locations and allow location access, or type your ZIP code
  • Confirm the address, phone number, and today's hours on the individual location page, since hours vary by whether the restaurant sits inside a mall or stands alone
  • Check if online ordering or delivery is listed as available for that specific address before you drive over
  • If you want a table without a long wait, log into Cheesecake Rewards and check whether that location accepts online reservations

The Cheesecake Factory is corporately operated in the U.S., meaning nearly every American location runs the same menu, hours structure, and reservation system rather than a franchise setup with local variation.

Reservations and wait times

Cheesecake Factory dining rooms fill up fast on weekend nights, and walk-in waits can run anywhere from a few minutes to a couple of hours at busy locations. Online reservations are only available to Cheesecake Rewards members, and even then only at participating locations for parties up to six guests. If you don't want to wait, joining Rewards before you leave the house is the single most useful step, since non-members are limited to walk-in seating at most restaurants.

Ordering options: dine-in, pickup, or delivery

Every U.S. location supports dine-in, and most also support online ordering for pickup and curbside, plus delivery through third-party apps.

  • Dine-in: Full table service, full menu, and the only way to order off the physical dessert case in person
  • Pickup: Order through the official website or app to skip delivery fees entirely
  • Curbside: Available at most locations when you select it during online checkout
  • Delivery: DoorDash is the official delivery partner, with Uber Eats and Grubhub also available at most locations. Linking a Cheesecake Rewards account to DoorDash can unlock member-specific discounts on delivery orders

Delivery menus sometimes run a slightly smaller selection than the in-restaurant menu, so if you want a specific dish, it's worth checking the delivery app's menu before ordering rather than assuming full parity.

Cheesecake Rewards

Cheesecake Rewards is free to join through the website or app and doesn't use a points system. Instead, members get a complimentary slice of cheesecake or layer cake on their birthday with any purchase, access to online reservations, occasional surprise offers like a discount on a set spend, and DoorDash-specific delivery deals. Signing up takes under a minute and only asks for your name, birthday, phone number, and email.

Dietary notes

Many dishes can be modified for vegetarian requests, and the SkinnyLicious section of the menu is built around lighter-calorie options rather than a specific diet label. Gluten-sensitive guests should ask staff directly at their location, since substitutions vary by dish and the kitchen doesn't operate as a dedicated gluten-free facility. Full allergen and ingredient breakdowns are listed on the official nutrition page for anyone with a specific restriction.

Catering and large groups

If you're feeding a group rather than visiting for a meal, the chain offers catering and whole cheesecakes for pickup in 6-inch, 7-inch, and 10-inch sizes, which serve better for parties than ordering individual slices. Contact your nearest location directly for lead times, since catering availability and order windows vary by restaurant.
