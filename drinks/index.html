Cheesecake Factory Beverage Menu: Prices, Calories & Every Drink Category (July 2026)

From specialty cocktails and frozen blends to iced teas, milkshakes, coffee, and non-alcoholic mocktails — every drink The Cheesecake Factory pours, with calories, ingredients, and pricing for July 2026. You'll also find nutrition callouts, lighter picks, and answers to the questions people actually ask before ordering.

Prices below are typical U.S. menu prices and can shift by a dollar or two depending on your location, since The Cheesecake Factory sets pricing restaurant by restaurant. Calorie counts come from the chain's standardized nutritional guide; recipes are made to order, so an actual pour can vary slightly from the listed value.

Quick answer

The Cheesecake Factory's bar and beverage program breaks into roughly nine groups: specialty cocktails, mojitos, margaritas, martinis and chilled drinks, beer and wine, non-alcoholic specialty sodas, Factory Iced Teas, frozen drinks and creamy milkshakes, and hot drinks and espresso. Zero-proof versions of several signature cocktails are also available. Most specialty cocktails run $9 to $14, mocktails and sodas land in the $6 to $7 range, milkshakes and smoothies run $9 to $11, and iced tea, lemonade, or soda by the glass is usually $4 to $6.

Non-alcoholic specialty sodas

Factory Iced Teas

Final thoughts

The Cheesecake Factory's beverage menu covers a lot more ground than most casual dining chains, spanning fresh-juice cocktails, a real espresso bar, housemade milkshakes, and a rotating beer and wine list that changes by location. If you're watching calories, the SkinnyLicious Mojito, brewed iced tea, and zero-proof cocktails are the safest picks, while the Oreo Milkshake and Factory Hot Chocolate sit at the far end of the scale. Since pricing and availability shift from one restaurant to the next, checking your specific location's online menu before you go is still the most reliable way to know exactly what you'll pay.
