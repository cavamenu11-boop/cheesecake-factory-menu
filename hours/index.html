Cheesecake Factory Hours: Complete July 2026 Guide

Here's everything worth knowing about Cheesecake Factory hours for July 2026: standard opening and closing times, weekend and brunch hours, happy hour windows, and the full holiday schedule for the rest of the year. You'll also find guidance on how hours shift by location type and how to confirm the exact hours for the restaurant you're planning to visit.

Hours at Cheesecake Factory are set by each individual location, so the times below reflect the typical schedule followed by most restaurants rather than a single company-wide rule. Mall locations, downtown restaurants, and stores in major tourist cities often run on their own timetable.

Quick answer: standard Cheesecake Factory hours

Most Cheesecake Factory restaurants open at 11:00 AM Monday through Saturday and at 10:00 AM on Sunday. Closing time is usually 10:00 PM Sunday through Thursday and 11:00 PM on Friday and Saturday.

Hours by meal: lunch, dinner, and brunch

Cheesecake Factory doesn't split its day into separate lunch and dinner menus. The full menu, including sandwiches, pastas, steaks, and cheesecakes, is available from open to close at every location. That means there's no cutoff time where certain dishes disappear from the menu once the lunch rush ends.

The one exception is brunch. On Saturdays and Sundays, many locations roll out a brunch menu that runs until roughly 2:00 PM, layering egg dishes, pancakes, and brunch cocktails on top of the standard lineup. After 2:00 PM, the restaurant shifts back to its regular all-day menu for the rest of the afternoon and evening.

Happy hour times

Happy hour runs Monday through Friday from 4:00 PM to 6:00 PM in the bar area. During this window, the restaurant offers discounted appetizers and drink specials, though the exact lineup of what's included can differ from one location to the next. Happy hour seating is generally limited to the bar and lounge area rather than the main dining room, so arriving early on a busy weeknight helps if you want a table there.

Do Cheesecake Factory hours vary by location?

Yes, and the differences can be significant depending on where the restaurant sits.

  • Mall-based restaurants often follow the mall's operating hours for the main entrance, though many keep a separate exterior door open later for guests who want to stay past mall closing. If a location sits inside a shopping center, it's worth checking the mall's hours alongside the restaurant's own listed hours
  • Major metro and tourist locations frequently stay open later than the standard schedule. Restaurants in cities like Las Vegas, New York, and Los Angeles have been known to serve until 12:30 AM on weekend nights, driven by heavier evening foot traffic in those markets
  • Suburban and standalone locations tend to stick closest to the standard 11:00 AM to 10:00 PM or 11:00 PM pattern shown in the table above

Holiday hours 2026

Cheesecake Factory stays open for most holidays on the calendar, since dining out is often part of how people celebrate. There are only two days each year when nearly every location shuts its doors completely.

Closed company-wide

  • Thanksgiving Day
  • Christmas Day

Restaurants close on these two days specifically so staff can spend the holiday with their own families, and this applies to essentially every U.S. location.

Open with normal or slightly adjusted hours

  • New Year's Day
  • Martin Luther King Jr. Day
  • Valentine's Day
  • Presidents Day
  • St. Patrick's Day
  • Easter Sunday (some locations, especially those inside malls, may run shortened hours)
  • Cinco de Mayo
  • Mother's Day
  • Memorial Day
  • Juneteenth
  • Father's Day
  • Independence Day (July 4th)
  • Labor Day
  • Columbus Day
  • Halloween
  • Veterans Day
  • Black Friday
  • Cyber Monday
  • New Year's Eve (many locations close a bit earlier than usual, often between 9:00 PM and 10:00 PM)

Christmas Eve is the one date on the "open" list that reliably runs on a shortened schedule. Most restaurants close somewhere between 6:00 PM and 8:00 PM rather than sticking to their normal closing time, so a late dinner plan on December 24 is risky without checking first.

A note worth remembering here: national or third-party hours sites, and even Google Maps, can lag behind when a holiday schedule changes at short notice. Calling the restaurant or checking its page on the official Cheesecake Factory website a day or two before a holiday visit is the safest way to avoid a wasted trip.

How to find exact hours for your local restaurant

Because hours are location-specific, the fastest way to get an exact answer is to look up your restaurant directly rather than relying on the general schedule above.

  • Go to the Cheesecake Factory locations page on the official website and search by city or zip code
  • Open your specific restaurant's page, which lists that location's current hours, including any temporary holiday adjustments
  • Call the restaurant directly if you need same-day confirmation, especially around a holiday
  • Check the Cheesecake Factory app, which also shows hours alongside reservation and ordering tools

Ordering hours: pickup, curbside, and delivery

Online ordering generally follows the restaurant's regular open and close times, and the full menu, including alcohol where it's legally permitted, is available for pickup. Orders can be placed through the Cheesecake Factory app, the website, by phone, or in person at the bakery counter. Many locations also offer curbside to-go service, where staff bring the order out to your car once you select that option during checkout.

Delivery is handled through DoorDash at most locations, and delivery availability generally mirrors the restaurant's dine-in hours, though cutoff times for the kitchen can run a little earlier than the posted closing time. On Thanksgiving and Christmas Day, when restaurants are closed, online ordering for that location is unavailable as well.

Final thoughts

Cheesecake Factory keeps a fairly consistent schedule at most locations: late morning openings, evening closings that stretch later on weekends, and an all-day menu with no separate breakfast cutoff. The real variable is location. Malls, resort towns, and major cities all run their own version of "standard hours." Thanksgiving and Christmas Day are the only closures you can count on everywhere, while Christmas Eve is the date most likely to catch visitors off guard with an early close. When in doubt, a quick call or app check before you head out saves the trip.
