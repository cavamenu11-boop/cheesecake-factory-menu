About this guide

The Cheesecake Factory menu features more than 250 scratch-made dishes across appetizers, burgers, pasta, chicken, seafood, steaks, salads, sandwiches, pizza, desserts, drinks, and over 30 signature cheesecake flavors. This guide brings every major menu category together in one place with updated 2026 price ranges, calorie information, and quick access to dedicated pages where you can browse each menu in full.

Because The Cheesecake Factory sets menu prices by location, there isn't a single nationwide price list. Instead of showing estimated or outdated prices, we compare real menu prices from restaurants and delivery listings across multiple U.S. cities to provide realistic price ranges. Calorie information comes from the company's published nutrition data, helping you compare menu items before dining in, ordering takeout, or placing a delivery order.

About The Cheesecake Factory

The Cheesecake Factory was founded in 1978 by David Overton in Beverly Hills, California, building on his mother Evelyn's cheesecake recipe, which she'd been selling out of a small bakery since the 1970s. What began as a family bakery has grown into one of the most recognizable casual-dining chains in the country, known for its massive menu, generous portions, and more than 30 rotating cheesecake flavors.
