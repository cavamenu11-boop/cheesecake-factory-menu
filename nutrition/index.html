Cheesecake Factory Nutrition Menu: Calories, Macros & Allergens (July 2026)

Calories across the Cheesecake Factory menu, category by category: appetizers, entrees, salads, sides, breakfast, desserts, and drinks, plus dedicated breakdowns for SkinnyLicious (under 590 calories), Gluten Free, and lower-carb and higher-protein picks. Figures below come from Cheesecake Factory's published nutrition data as compiled by independent nutrition trackers, current as of July 2026.

Because recipes are prepared to order and menus are updated periodically, exact calories can shift slightly by location and over time. For the precise, real-time figure on any single dish, Cheesecake Factory's own nutrition calculator on its website is the most current source. Numbers below are meant for planning and comparison, not as a substitute for that tool.

See the full Cheesecake Factory menu for prices alongside these calorie figures, or check Happy Hour for lighter, discounted options.

How to check exact nutrition before you order

  • Cheesecake Factory's official nutrition calculator lets you search any dish and see calories, fat, carbs, sodium, and other values for the specific version currently served

Lower-carb options

Cheesecake Factory doesn't run a dedicated low-carb menu the way it does for gluten-free or SkinnyLicious, but several existing dishes are naturally lower in carbohydrates.

  • Grilled proteins without pasta or bread: Chargrilled New York Steak, Grilled Rib-Eye, Filet Mignon, Fresh Grilled Salmon, SkinnyLicious Grilled Steak Medallions
  • Salad add-ons instead of a burger or sandwich: grilled chicken, salmon, or shrimp over greens keeps carbs to what's in the dressing and vegetables
  • Low Carb Cheesecake: the one dessert on the menu specifically formulated with reduced carbohydrates, at 610 calories per slice
  • Vegetable sides over starch sides: broccoli, green beans, and sauteed spinach in place of fries, rice, or mashed potatoes

Swapping a burger's bun for a lettuce wrap or ordering a protein "protein style" over salad are common in-restaurant requests, though Cheesecake Factory doesn't list an official calorie or carb figure for these customizations, so ask your server for the closest published equivalent.

Higher-protein options

Grilled proteins and protein-forward entrees are scattered across several sections rather than grouped into one menu, so this list pulls the standout picks together.

  • SkinnyLicious Grilled Salmon (570 cal): a full grilled salmon entree under 590 calories
  • SkinnyLicious Grilled Steak Medallions (440 cal): the lightest steak entree on the whole menu
  • Grilled Chicken salad add-on (220 cal): a straightforward way to add protein to any salad without a heavy sauce
  • Grilled Salmon salad add-on (220 cal) and Chargrilled Steak salad add-on (200 cal): similar logic, protein without the starch or sauce that usually comes with a full entree
  • Filet Mignon, Gluten Free (860 cal): protein-dense without pasta, bread, or heavy sauce
  • SkinnyLicious Turkey & Avocado Sandwich (550 cal): protein from turkey rather than fried chicken or beef

Cheesecake Factory doesn't publish protein-in-grams for every dish on its public-facing menu descriptions, only through the nutrition calculator on a per-item basis, so treat this list as a starting point for what to search on the official tool rather than a final ranked answer.

Vegetarian & vegan options

Vegetarian dishes are spread across multiple categories rather than isolated into one section. Fully vegan options are more limited, since many vegetarian dishes still include dairy or egg.

  • Vegetarian appetizers: Edamame, Crispy Brussels Sprouts, Korean Fried Cauliflower, Sweet Corn Tamale Cakes, Stuffed Mushrooms
  • Vegetarian entrees: Veggie Burger, SkinnyLicious Veggie Burger, Four Cheese Pasta, Margherita Flatbread
  • Vegan-friendly starting points: the Impossible Burger can be ordered without cheese or mayo-based sauces, and several salads can be built vegan by removing cheese and swapping to an oil-and-vinegar-style dressing

What to confirm in person: shared fryers, butter-based cooking, and cheese used across otherwise plant-based dishes are common at full-service chains, so it's worth asking your server directly rather than assuming a dish is vegan from the menu description alone.

What to know before you go

  • Portions are built for sharing or leftovers. Many entrees, especially pasta, run 1,500+ calories because the plate is meant to serve as two meals for a lot of guests, not because the recipe itself is unusually rich
  • The bread basket is not included in any of the figures above. Each roll runs roughly 150 calories, so a few pieces before your meal add up before your entree even arrives
  • Ask for sauces and dressings on the side. This is consistently the single biggest lever across salads, pasta, and sandwiches, often cutting 100–300 calories without changing the actual dish you ordered
  • SkinnyLicious and Gluten Free are separate systems. A dish can be one, both, or neither, so check both labels if you have overlapping needs
  • Recipes change. Menu items and their nutrition info are updated periodically, so treat any number here as a strong reference point and confirm anything critical, especially for allergies, through the official nutrition calculator or your server

Final thoughts

Cheesecake Factory's menu spans an unusually wide calorie range for one restaurant, from 100-calorie edamame to pasta dishes north of 2,000 calories on a single plate. SkinnyLicious and Gluten Free give real, structured ways to eat lighter or manage a dietary need without leaving the restaurant's usual flavors behind, and small adjustments like dressing on the side or a salad swap for fries go further here than at most chains given how large the base portions run. The official nutrition calculator remains the best source for the exact figure on whatever you're about to order, especially if you're managing an allergy or a specific health goal.
