Cheesecake Factory Seafood Menu: Prices, Calories & What to Order (2026)

Last updated: September 30, 2026

If you want the classic, order the Herb Crusted Filet of Salmon or the Miso Salmon. Both are the most consistently praised seafood dishes on the menu, richer and heavier (1,300+ calories). If you want something lighter, skip the Fish & Seafood section entirely and order the SkinnyLicious® Grilled Salmon or the Pan Seared Branzino with Lemon Butter, both under 900 calories. Which one is right for you depends on which of those two things matters more today. The rest of this guide breaks down why. If you'd rather browse everything the restaurant serves first, the full menu covers every category, not just seafood.

One correction worth making here: some other menu guides describe Miso Salmon itself as "hand-battered and deep-fried." That description matches a fried item elsewhere on the menu, not Miso Salmon, which is grilled or broiled with a miso glaze. If you're avoiding fried food specifically, Miso Salmon is not the dish to rule out for that reason.

The Cheesecake Factory does not guarantee any dish is fully allergen-free due to shared kitchen equipment. Always confirm specifics with your server if you have a serious allergy. Why seafood prices vary by location

Cheesecake Factory doesn't run one national price list; pricing is set per restaurant. That's why you'll see different numbers for the same dish (like Fresh Grilled Salmon) depending on which site or which city you check. Neither number is necessarily wrong; they're just different locations. The only reliable way to get an exact price is to check your specific location's menu or ask when you order.

One thing that is consistent: most Fish & Seafood entrées are also available in a lunch portion on weekdays before roughly 4 to 5 PM, at a meaningfully lower price than the dinner portion of the same dish. If price matters more than portion size, that's the move. The same logic applies at the bar: the Happy Hour menu runs weekdays from 4 to 6 PM with discounted appetizers and cocktails, so pairing a lunch-portion seafood entrée with an early Happy Hour visit is the cheapest way to order from this section.

Final thoughts

If you're deciding right now: pick Herb Crusted Filet of Salmon or Miso Salmon for the classic, richer experience, or SkinnyLicious Grilled Salmon or Grilled Branzino if calories are the priority. If you have a gluten or dairy restriction, confirm the glaze and sauce with your server rather than assuming from the dish name. And if the price you see here doesn't match what you're quoted in person, that's location pricing, not an error. Check the lunch portion before you order if cost is the deciding factor.
