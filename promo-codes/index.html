Cheesecake Factory deals without a code (automatic discounts)

These apply automatically. Nothing to type in at checkout.

National Cheesecake Day Deal: 50% Off Any Slice, July 30, 2026

  • All 30+ cheesecake flavors qualify, no minimum spend
  • Limit one discounted slice per guest, and each person must be present at the table
  • No membership or app check-in required
  • New flavor debuting the same day, the Brownie Crunch Choc-a-Lot Cheesecake, is sold at regular price, it's not discounted itself
  • For every slice of that new flavor sold through July 29, 2027, the company donates 25 cents to Feeding America. That's a charity tie-in, not an extra discount

Cheesecake Rewards Birthday Discount: Free Slice Offer

  • Must be attached to another purchase, it's not a standalone freebie
  • Redeem by telling your server you're a member, or by staying logged in for an online order
  • Requires a free Cheesecake Rewards account with your birthday on file

Cheesecake Rewards loyalty program: how points-free savings work

  • Free to join at cheesecakerewards.com or in the app
  • No points system. Instead, members get periodic "surprise and delight" offers plus the guaranteed birthday slice
  • Dining in: tell your server you're a member and give the phone number on your account
  • Ordering online or in-app: stay logged in so eligible rewards apply automatically
  • Offer timing and value aren't predictable since there's no visible points balance
  • Some franchise or international locations don't participate, so it's worth confirming with your specific restaurant
  • Sign-up requires: name, email, and a U.S. mobile number (used to deliver offers by text)

Cheesecake Factory DoorDash promo codes and free delivery offers

The Cheesecake Factory's delivery discounts run mostly through DoorDash, not through a promo code field on the brand's own site.

  • 10GIFT ($10 off $50+ orders): expired, ran February 2021 as part of a Feeding America donation campaign. Still referenced on some coupon sites, don't use it
  • General DoorDash new-customer/seasonal credits: DoorDash account-level offers, not brand-specific. They change often, check your DoorDash app for what's currently active
  • Rewards-linked DoorDash offers: available for some members once a Cheesecake Rewards account is linked to DoorDash, no code required

Cheesecake Factory student discount code

  • No corporate student discount program found on the official site, rewards terms, or recent company announcements
  • Best available option for students: join Cheesecake Rewards (free) or split a full-size entree, since portions run large

Cheesecake Factory military and first responder discount code

  • No corporate-wide military or first responder discount is listed in official terms
  • Some individual locations have reportedly offered a discretionary discount (commonly cited around 10%) to active-duty personnel, but this is not a documented chain policy and can't be confirmed as consistent
  • Recommended: call your specific location ahead of your visit to ask directly

Cheesecake Factory app-only deals and coupon codes

  • Available for iOS and Android
  • Main functions: view surprise offers, manage your birthday reward, make reservations, place pickup/delivery orders while logged in
  • No separate app-exclusive sign-up code or discount beyond standard Cheesecake Rewards benefits was found
  • Joining Cheesecake Rewards, not just downloading the app, is what unlocks these features

How to redeem a Cheesecake Factory promo code, step by step

Dine-in (National Cheesecake Day or Rewards birthday slice)

  1. Get seated and wait for your server to take your order.
  2. Mention you're a Rewards member, or that you're there for the current promotion, before ordering.
  3. For a Rewards offer, provide the phone number linked to your account.

Pickup or delivery via thecheesecakefactory.com or the app

  1. Log in before you start your order, since eligible Rewards offers apply only while logged in.
  2. Add items and go to checkout.
  3. Check your order summary for the applied reward before paying.

DoorDash

  1. Open the DoorDash app or site and search for The Cheesecake Factory.
  2. Any active DoorDash-wide or brand-specific promotion should populate automatically or show as a banner.
  3. Confirm the discount on your total before submitting.

Stacking and combining Cheesecake Factory discount codes

  • Cheesecake Rewards offers and DoorDash promotions are separate systems and generally aren't designed to combine
  • A DoorDash first-order credit is a DoorDash account benefit, not something The Cheesecake Factory controls, so whether it stacks with anything else depends on DoorDash's checkout logic that day
  • National Cheesecake Day's half-price slice is a standalone, dine-in-only event and isn't described as stackable with Rewards offers
  • Always check your total before paying rather than assuming multiple discounts applied
