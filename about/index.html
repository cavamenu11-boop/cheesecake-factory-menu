Who We Are

Cheesecake Factory Menus is an independent, unofficial guide built for people who want fast, reliable answers about The Cheesecake Factory's menu — prices, calories, hours, and what's actually on offer — without digging through app screens or outdated forum posts.

What This Site Is (and Isn't)

We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or operated by The Cheesecake Factory, Inc. or any of its subsidiaries. All trademarks, logos, and brand names referenced on this site are the property of their respective owners and are used only for identification and informational purposes.

Our Approach

Because The Cheesecake Factory prices its menu by location, we don't publish a single guessed number. Instead, we compile real prices from restaurants and delivery platforms across multiple U.S. cities to give realistic ranges, and we pair that with the company's published nutrition data for calorie information. Our goal is accuracy and usefulness, not just page count.

Keeping Things Current

Menus, prices, and offerings change often. We review and update this site periodically, but for anything time-sensitive — same-day pricing, current promotions, allergen specifics — we always recommend confirming directly with your local restaurant or the official Cheesecake Factory website.

How We Verify Prices and Calories

We do not publish a single guessed nationwide price. Prices are compiled from restaurant menus and delivery listings across multiple U.S. cities and shown as realistic ranges, because the same dish can cost different amounts in different locations. Where a price still varies too much to give a useful range, we say so on the page instead of inventing a number. Calorie figures are paired with the company's published nutrition data.

What You Will Find Here

Beyond the main menu, this guide covers appetizers, pasta, seafood, salads, drinks and cheesecake desserts, plus Happy Hour, promo codes, hours, locations, nutrition, allergen and gluten-free guides, so you can check price, calories and timing before you order.

Contact Us
