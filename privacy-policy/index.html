Privacy Policy

How Cheesecake Factory Menus collects, uses, and protects information.

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains what information Cheesecake Factory Menus collects, how it is used, and the choices available to you. This site is an independent, unofficial guide and is not affiliated with The Cheesecake Factory, Inc.

Information We Collect

We do not require account creation or collect personal information to browse this site. If you use the contact form, we collect the name, email address, and message you provide, solely to respond to your inquiry.

Like most websites, we automatically collect limited technical information when you visit, such as your browser type, device type, approximate location (derived from IP address), pages viewed, and time spent on the site. This is used in aggregate to understand site usage and is not used to personally identify you.

Cookies

This site may use cookies and similar technologies to remember preferences, understand how visitors use the site, and support analytics or advertising. You can disable cookies through your browser settings; doing so may affect some site functionality.

Analytics

We may use third-party analytics services, such as Google Analytics, to understand how visitors interact with this site. These services may use cookies and collect data such as pages visited and time on site. This information is used in aggregate and is not used to personally identify individual visitors.

Advertising

This site may display advertising, including through Google AdSense or similar third-party ad networks. These networks may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising through your browser settings or via Google's Ads Settings.

Third-Party Links

This site links to third-party sites, including the official Cheesecake Factory website and food delivery platforms. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those external sites, and we encourage you to review their privacy policies separately.

Children's Privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information submitted through this site, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. Continued use of the site after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out through our Contact page.
